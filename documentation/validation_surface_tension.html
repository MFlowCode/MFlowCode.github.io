Validation: Surface Tension

Surface tension: Laplace pressure jump of a static drop

Status: validated (static 2D drop). Code: master at 5838b641c. Example: examples/2D_laplace_pressure_jump. Last run: 2026-11-24. See also Validation.

Purpose

Surface tension (surface_tension, the capillary model of Schmidmayer et al. [78] with a color function) must balance the pressure jump across a curved interface at rest. A 2D drop in equilibrium has the exact jump \(\Delta p = \sigma/R\); the residual flow (spurious currents) measures how well the discrete capillary force and the pressure gradient balance.

Setup

Item Value
Geometry quarter of a 2D drop centred on the corner of [0, 0.375]^2 m (symmetry planes at x = 0 and y = 0, extrapolation on the far faces); r0 = 0.15 m; N x N cells
Fluids water (stiffened gas, gamma 2.1, pi_inf 1e6 Pa, 1000 kg/m^3) in air (gamma 1.4, 1 kg/m^3), 1e5 Pa, at rest; 1e-9 of the other fluid in each pure region; interface smoothed (smooth_coeff 0.95)
Surface tension sigma = 8 N/m, so sigma/r0 = 53.33 Pa (5.3e-4 of p0)
Numerics 6-equation model, WENO5-M with mp_weno and weno_avg, HLLC, SSP-RK3, dt = 5e-6 s x 50/N; t = 0.5 s (about 60 acoustic crossings of the drop)
Reference \(\Delta p = \sigma/R\), R the equivalent radius of the water area, \(\sqrt{4A/\pi}\) with \(A = \sum \alpha_\mathrm{water}\,\Delta x\Delta y\)
Measured mean p where alpha_water > 0.999 minus mean p where alpha_water < 0.001 and r > 1.5 R, averaged over the last 20 saves; the largest speed in the window; check_laplace.py

Assessment

  • The pressure jump matches \(\sigma/R\) to 1.0 % at 25 cells, 0.01 % at 50 and 0.25 % at 100 cells across the box (10, 20 and 40 cells per drop radius). The error does not decrease monotonically past 50 cells: at 100 cells the jump still varies by 0.9 % over the averaging window, which is larger than its error, so the 50 and 100 cell values agree within that unsteadiness.
  • R moves by 0.4 % from the initial radius at 25 cells and by 0.03 % at 100 (smoothing of the initial interface); measured against the initial radius the error is below 0.6 % on every grid.
  • The A100 and CPU runs at 50 cells agree to 3e-5 in the jump.
  • The spurious currents are 0.04 to 0.05 m/s, 16 to 23 % of the capillary speed, and do not fall with the grid.

Known limitations

  • Only the static drop is checked. The oscillation frequency of a perturbed drop (Rayleigh and Lamb) and capillary waves were not run: no example sets them up.
  • The spurious currents (16 to 23 % of the capillary speed) do not fall with the grid; the jump is averaged over 20 saves to reduce their pressure signature, which at 100 cells is still 0.9 % of the jump.
  • 2D only; a 3D drop ( \(\Delta p = 2\sigma/R\)) was not run.

Reproduce

./mfc.sh run examples/2D_laplace_pressure_jump/case.py -t pre_process simulation post_process -n 1 -- -N 50
python3 examples/2D_laplace_pressure_jump/check_laplace.py examples/2D_laplace_pressure_jump --case-args "-N 50"

case.py takes -N (default 50), --tend (0.5 s) and --saves (100). The 50-cell run takes 27 minutes on one CPU core and 22 minutes on an A100 with --case-optimization (the run is small enough that the GPU does not help); 100 cells were run on the GPU only. The example is on the test-suite skip list; no test was added.
