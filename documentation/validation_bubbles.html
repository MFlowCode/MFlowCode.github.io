Validation: Bubble Dynamics

Bubble dynamics: Keller-Miksis collapse in a uniform bubbly liquid

Status: validated (Euler-Euler bubbles, one bubble size, polytropic gas). Code: master at 5838b641c. Example: examples/0D_bubblecollapse_adap. Last run: 2026-11-24. See also Validation.

Purpose

The Euler-Euler bubble model (bubbles_euler) carries a bubble radius and wall velocity per cell and advances them with a bubble ODE, here Keller-Miksis Keller and Miksis [40], sub-stepped adaptively (adap_dt). In a uniform periodic mixture every cell holds the same bubble, so the radius history can be compared with the ODE integrated on its own. This checks the ODE, the adaptive sub-steps and the coupling to the mixture pressure, and measures the collapse time against Rayleigh's Lord Rayleigh [46].

Setup

Item Value
Geometry 1D periodic, 31 cells (uniform state: no fluxes)
Liquid water, stiffened gas (gamma 7.1, pi_inf 306 MPa), 1000 kg/m^3, mu 1.002e-3 Pa s, sigma 0.07275 N/m, p_v 2338.8 Pa
Bubbles R0 = 50 um, at rest, equilibrium at p0 = 8236 Pa, polytropic air (gamma 1.4), one size (nb = 1), void fraction 1e-5
Drive mixture pressure 1000 p0 at t = 0
Numerics 5-equation model, WENO5-M, HLLC, SSP-RK3, fixed dt = 1e-4 t0 (t0 = R0/sqrt(p0/rho_l) = 17.4 us), adap_dt_tol 1e-4; 501 steps, a save every step
Reference Keller-Miksis in the units of the case (the same equation as m_bubbles.fpp: polytropic gas with surface tension and viscosity, liquid sound speed of the initial state), SciPy LSODA, rtol 1e-11; driven by (a) the constant initial pressure, (b) MFC's cell pressure interpolated between saves
Measured R = nR001/n from the saves, by check_collapse.py

Assessment

  • Driven by its own cell pressure, the ODE reproduces MFC's radius to 9e-5 R0 through the collapse and rebound (1.7 t_c), and the result does not move with the sub-step tolerance or the time step. The bubble ODE and its adaptive integration are verified.
  • Against the constant-pressure ODE the radius differs by 4e-3 R0. This is the two-way coupling, not an error: the mixture pressure falls by 0.27 % as the void fraction collapses from 1e-5 to 3e-8 (stiff liquid, fixed mixture density), and the bubble sees the lower pressure.
  • The collapse takes 1.041 to 1.043 Rayleigh times: the gas, surface tension, viscosity and liquid compressibility of Keller-Miksis lengthen it by 4 % over the empty-cavity value. The time of the minimum is a parabola through the three saves around it and moves by 0.1 % with the save interval; the sampled minimum radius (0.04 to 0.05 R0) is not resolved by the saves.

Known limitations

  • One bubble size, polytropic gas, no wave propagation. Polydisperse and QBMM closures (examples/1D_qbmm), the non-polytropic thermal model, bubble screens with acoustic coupling and the Lagrangian sub-grid bubbles (bubbles_lagrange) are not checked here. The Lagrangian model requires thermal = 3 with the non-polytropic gas model, so the polytropic ODE above does not apply to it.
  • Only the Keller-Miksis model is run; Rayleigh-Plesset and Gilmore are not.

Reproduce

./mfc.sh run examples/0D_bubblecollapse_adap/case.py -t pre_process simulation post_process -n 1
python3 examples/0D_bubblecollapse_adap/check_collapse.py examples/0D_bubblecollapse_adap

Options of case.py: --dt, --adap-tol. The suite runs the example at a reduced size (every example not on the skip list in toolchain/mfc/test/cases.py is registered with at most 25 cells and 50 steps), which guards the code path; the checker validates the physics. No test was added.
