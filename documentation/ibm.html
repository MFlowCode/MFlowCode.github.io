Immersed Boundaries

This page is a map of the immersed-boundary (IB) options. The parameters and their constraints are in Immersed Boundary Patches of the case-file documentation; this page only says which option does what and where it is checked. Enable the model with ib = T and num_ibs; each body is a patch_ib(i) entry whose geometry is listed in Immersed Boundary Patch Types.

Motion

Option Effect
moving_ibm = 0 fixed body
moving_ibm = 1 one-way coupling: the body moves at its prescribed rate and acts on the fluid, which does not act back
moving_ibm = 2 two-way coupling: the body moves under pressure, viscous and body forces (bf_x, bf_y, bf_z)
collision_model = 1 soft-sphere collisions between circles or spheres and with walls; coefficient_of_restitution, collision_time, collision_temporal_resolution, ib_coefficient_of_friction
ib_friction_slip_eps regularises the friction direction at small slip speed, so contacts split across MPI ranks agree with a single rank

Moving-body kinematics and the restitution of a wall collision are checked in Validation.

Wall treatment

Option Effect
ib_second_order_vel, ib_ip_min_dist linear ghost-velocity extrapolation; minimum image-point distance
ib_ip_interp = 2 triangular-shaped-cloud image-point interpolation instead of inverse-distance weights
ib_wall_order = 2 two-probe quadratic wall-normal ghost velocity at no-slip walls
ib_slip_ccst, ib_smooth_contact, ib_uncover_fill slip-wall ghost state, HLLC contact blending in the viscous work, fill of cells a moving body uncovers
weno_Re_flux, ib_visc_compact compact velocity gradient on the viscous faces within two cells of a body
ib_mass_relax returns the spurious gas mass of a body over about N steps; the default resolves at start-up (see the parameter entry)

Each option has a default that leaves existing results unchanged, except ib_mass_relax, whose default depends on whether a body moves. Drag and Nusselt number of a cylinder: Validation: Circular Cylinder Drag and Nusselt Number. Interpolation against an exact conduction solution: Isothermal Conduction Annulus: Image-Point Interpolation. Slip walls (mass budget, exact vortex, known failures): Validation: Slip Immersed Walls. Lumped cooling and the wall conduction column: Validation: Solid-Wall Thermal Models.

Heat transfer and chemistry

thermal_bc selects the wall condition: zero-gradient (0), prescribed Twall (1), reacting-surface energy balance (2), lumped solid with an evolving temperature (3). wall_column adds a sub-grid conduction column to a fixed isothermal circle or sphere, and ib_flux_wall sets the face fluxes of a reacting wall from its surface solve. Examples: examples/2D_ibm_lumped_wall, examples/2D_ibm_wall_mass_budget, examples/2D_ibm_conduction_annulus.

Output and restart

  • ib_force_wrt writes ib_forces.dat (force history); ib_heat_wrt writes ib_heat.dat (heat history); ib_surface_wrt writes surface-point records for a chemistry run.
  • ib_state_wrt writes the IB state as a point mesh in post-processing.
  • The IB state is saved under restart_data/ (ib_state_*.dat; the conduction temperature of ghost points in ib_tcond_<step>.dat).
