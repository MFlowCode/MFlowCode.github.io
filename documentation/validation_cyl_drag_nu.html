Validation: Circular Cylinder Drag and Nusselt Number

Circular cylinder, Re 20-100: drag, wake, shedding and heat transfer

Status: partially validated. Code: master at 615348a25. Example: examples/2D_ibm_cylinder_drag_nu. Last run: 2026-11-21.

Purpose

A ghost-cell immersed boundary with chemistry-based thermodynamics and transport is the code path of particle-resolved reacting runs. This checks the drag from the face-flux force of ib_force_wrt (it replaced a volume integral that missed part of the friction drag, #2013), the wake and shedding frequency, and the Nusselt number from the face heat of ib_heat_wrt, against references and against a force that does not use the immersed-boundary code.

Setup

Item Value
Geometry 2D cylinder (patch_ib geometry 2), centred off the symmetry planes by (dx/3, dx/5) to seed shedding
Flow M = 0.2, Re_D = 20, 40, 100, 300 K, 1 atm, dry air (h2o2.yaml: O2 0.21, N2 0.79 by mole), chemistry on for thermodynamics and mixture-averaged transport, reactions off; constant \(\mu\) from the free stream
Grid 10 and 20 cells/D, uniform; domain [-15 D, 25 D] x [-16 D, 16 D] (blockage 3.1 %); Re 100: [-8 D, 16 D] x [-8 D, 8 D] (6.25 %)
Boundaries subsonic characteristic inflow, characteristic outflow holding the free-stream pressure (extrapolation at the outflow lets the far-field pressure creep up), extrapolated lateral faces
Wall fixed, no slip, thermal_bc = 1, Twall = T_inf (drag runs) or 1.1 T_inf (heat runs)
Numerics WENO5-M, HLLC, SSP-RK3, weno_Re_flux, fd_order = 4, adaptive step with CFL min(0.5, 0.4 Re/N) and chem_params%transport_dt; ib_ip_interp = 1 (the default)
Length \(t U/D\) = 50 (Re 20, 40), 120 (Re 100); averages over the last 20 %
Hardware AMD MI300A, OpenMP offload, double precision

Reference data

  • Dennis and Chang [DennisChang1970:] Cd 2.05 (Re 20) and 1.52 (Re 40); L/D 0.94 and 2.35. Fornberg [Fornberg1980:] Cd 2.00 and 1.50 (values as compiled in the literature; the original tables were not re-read).
  • Henderson [31] (Cd 1.35) and Park et al. [61] (Cd 1.33, Cl amplitude 0.332, St 0.165) at Re 100.
  • Williamson [Williamson1989:] St = -3.3265/Re + 0.1816 + 1.6e-4 Re (0.1643 at Re 100).
  • Nu: Lange et al. [38] (2D DNS fit, Pr = 0.7) and Churchill and Bernstein [14] (evaluated at the film Pr = 0.712).

Quantities

  • Cd_IB: \(\langle F_x\rangle/(\tfrac12\rho U^2 D)\) from D/ib_forces.dat.
  • Cd_CV: the same from a steady momentum balance on the square of half-width 1, 2 or 4 D, from the saved fields (check_cylinder.py); it drops the unsteady and acoustic terms.
  • L/D: first u = 0 crossing on the wake centreline behind the rear stagnation point, last save.
  • St from the dominant frequency of the lift history (second half of the run).
  • Nu = \(-(Q_\mathrm{hot}-Q_\mathrm{cold})\,D/(\pi D\,k_f\,(T_w-T_\infty))\) with Q the heat the body takes (D/ib_heat.dat, face fluxes), \(k_f\) at the film temperature, and the cold (Tw = T_inf) twin removing the viscous heating of the wall.

Assessment

  • The face-flux force agrees with the momentum balance (box 2 D) to 0.1 % at Re 20 and 0.1-0.3 % at Re 40 at 20 cells/D, and with the references to within 1.0 % at Re 20 and 40 and 0.3-1.8 % at Re 100. It moves toward the reference from 10 to 20 cells/D (Cd changes by 2.4 % at Re 20 and 1.4 % at Re 40).
  • The Strouhal number is +1.5 % against Williamson at 20 cells/D.
  • Nu is within 1.4 % of Lange at Re 20 and 40 and 3.2 % low at Re 100.
  • Status is partially validated: 40 cells per diameter, the grid-convergence order and Re 100 on the larger domain were not measured here.

Known limitations

  • L/D is first order and low (0.818 against 0.94 at Re 20, 2.064 against 2.35 at Re 40: 13 % and 12 % low at 20 cells/D, 23 % and 18 % low at 10). The no-slip ghost value is the wall value, not a reflection, so the effective wall sits up to a cell inside the surface (#2014); ib_wall_order = 2 is the option for it and was not run here.
  • Not re-measured (cost, about 5 GPU-hours per run on the 40 D x 32 D domain): 40 cells per diameter, and Re 100 on the domain of Re 20 and 40.
  • Re 100 uses a 6.25 % blockage; Cd_CV depends on the box there (1.302 for 4 D, which comes within 4 D of the inflow and lateral faces).
  • Nu varies by 2-4 % between correlations. The face-heat Nu carries no wall-gradient fit: an earlier drift of Nu with refinement at Re 40 came from fitting the wall gradient of the temperature field, not from the solver.
  • The film Prandtl number of dry air in h2o2.yaml is 0.712; Lange's fit is for 0.7.

Reproduce

./mfc.sh run examples/2D_ibm_cylinder_drag_nu/case.py -t pre_process simulation post_process -n 1 --gpu -- \
--silo --Re 40 --cells_per_D 20 --xu 15 --xd 25 --hy 16 --steps 0 --tstar 50
./mfc.sh run examples/2D_ibm_cylinder_drag_nu/case.py -t pre_process simulation post_process -n 1 --gpu -- \
--silo --Re 40 --cells_per_D 20 --xu 15 --xd 25 --hy 16 --steps 0 --tstar 50 --tw_ratio 1.1
python3 examples/2D_ibm_cylinder_drag_nu/check_cylinder.py <cold run> --hot <hot run>

Re 100: --Re 100 --tstar 120 --xu 8 --xd 16 --hy 8. A 20 cells/D run at Re 40 takes about 40 minutes on one MI300A (four runs ran concurrently on four GPUs). The test suite runs the CI-sized case (--tw_ratio 1.1, 16 cells/D, 200 steps) as 2D -> Chemistry -> IBM -> Cylinder Drag Nu.
