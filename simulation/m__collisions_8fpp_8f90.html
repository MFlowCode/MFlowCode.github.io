subroutine m_collisions::s_detect_ib_collisions (gps, ib_markers, num_gps, num_considered_collisions)
 Uses ghost-point information to find IB pairs that may be colliding, effectively an optimized nearest-neighbor search: every ghost point with another patch's marker within 2 cells gives a candidate pair. Both patches record it: the rank that owns the contact point may hold interior ghost points of only one of them, so one-sided detection can leave that rank blind to a contact within a cell of its boundary. The many duplicate candidates are dropped on the GPU: each candidate is mapped to its sorted neighborhood pair (a, b) and claims that pair's bit in pair_bits with an atomic OR, and only the thread that flips the bit appends the pair. Bits are cleared by s_clear_pair_bits after the forces. A pair's periodic image is taken from the claiming candidate; two different images of one pair would need a domain about two particles across.

integer, dimension(:), allocatable m_collisions::pair_bits
 One bit per unordered pair (a < b) of this rank's IBs (local + neighborhood indices, a triangular index over pair_bits_capacity): the ghost-point candidate pass claims each pair with an atomic OR, so only the first thread to find a pair records it and duplicates are dropped on the GPU. Bits are cleared after the forces are applied, so the set is empty between calls. Sized from num_ibs and regrown with it; memory is quadratic in the body count (1e4 bodies take 6 MB).
