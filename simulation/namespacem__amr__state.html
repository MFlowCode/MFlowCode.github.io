 Pack (dir=+1) / unpack (dir=-1) the fine cells of slot's q_cons over [dlo:dhi] in dim d (interior transverse, all sys_size) in a fixed order, so a packer and unpacker with matching extents align cell-for-cell. On GPU only this near-seam slab moves device<->host.

pure real(wp) function f_amr_quartic_half (q, xi)
 Average over one half (xi < 0 left, > 0 right; |xi| = 1/4) of the middle of five cell averages q: the conservative quartic, or MC-limited linear where either half leaves the range of the three nearest averages widened by their smallest second difference. Both halves are tested so the two children share one reconstruction (their mean stays q(0)).
