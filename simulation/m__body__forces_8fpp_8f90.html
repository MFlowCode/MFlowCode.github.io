impure subroutine, public m_body_forces::s_update_mean_sources (q_cons_vf, q_t_sf, t_step)
 Once per step, fluid-cell averages driving the mean sources (sums over fluid cells). Mean pressure gradient: bulk mass flux Q_i = sum(rho u_i dV)/sum(dV), controlled to mean_mflux(i) by -dP/dx; logged to mean_dpdx.dat. Thermostat: mass-averaged temperature T_b = sum(rho T dV)/sum(rho dV), controlled to mean_temp by the heat source with capacity rho c_v (fluid mean); logged to mean_temp.dat with the mixing-cup T_m = sum(rho u_1 T dV)/sum(rho u_1 dV) and the fluid volume.
