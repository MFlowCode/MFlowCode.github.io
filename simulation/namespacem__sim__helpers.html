 Sum over directions of 1/spacing^2 at cell (j, k, l). An explicit diffusion with diffusivity D is RK-stable for D*dt*sum <= 2.51/4 (RK3; 2/4 for RK1 and RK2), so the diffusive CFL numbers are D*dt*sum.
