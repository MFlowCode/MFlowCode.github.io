Detailed Description

Real fields of a particle MPI slot, as (names, indices, count) in pack order. Used by both pack and unpack routines to ensure.

Per-particle arrays as (name, type, extents after the particle index). State persists across stages and moves with the particle;

Euler-Lagrange solid particle solver with two-way coupling.

Tracks non-deformable solid particles in compressible flow using Gaussian volume-averaging (Maeda & Colonius, J. Computational Physics, 361, 2018). Supports multiple drag correlations, pressure gradient and added mass forces. Derived from the m_bubbles_EL module. Kernel functions are in m_particles_EL_kernels.
