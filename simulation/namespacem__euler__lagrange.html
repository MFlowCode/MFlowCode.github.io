Detailed Description

Utilities shared by the Euler-Lagrange bubble and solid-particle solvers: cell location, domain and cell-volume helpers, boundary handling, RK stages and Fornberg gradients. Their file I/O is in m_data_output (writes) and m_start_up (reads).
