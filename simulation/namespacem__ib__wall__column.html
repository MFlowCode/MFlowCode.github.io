Detailed Description

Sub-grid wall conduction column of an isothermal immersed wall (patch_ibwall_column): state and 1D numerics.

A linear ghost mirror carries at most about 2 k dT/h, while a wall layer thinner than a cell carries k dT/(sqrt(pi) delta): a cold particle in hot gas takes up too little heat early on. Each first-layer ghost of such a wall carries a 1D low-Mach conduction column along the wall normal, from the wall (T_w) to s_m = max(3h, L) (T from the gas), with the body's curvature. Its step-mean wall flux reaches the gas as one equivalent conduction temperature per ghost (m_ibm).

Advance one column over dt and return its step-mean wall heat flux into the body q, per unit wall area. Low-Mach energy equation of gas at rest at an isothermal wall, frozen composition, rho = rcoef/T (rcoef = p W/R_u): 

rho c_p (dT/dt + u dT/ds) = (1/A) d/ds (A lambda dT/ds),   d(A u)/ds = d(A lambda dT/ds)/ds / (rcoef c_p),   u(0) = 0

with T(0) = Tw and T(sm) = Tm. Finite volumes on the stretched cells; advection central where the cell Peclet number is below 1, else upwind. In time, wc_nsub substeps of the L-stable, stiffly accurate 2-stage SDIRK (gamma = 1 - 1/sqrt 2), coefficients lagged per substep, u from a diffusion-only implicit predictor. Both stages are implicit, so a step start does not ring into the wall flux. lam_t and cp_t tabulate lambda(T) and c_p(T) from Tlo in steps dTs.
