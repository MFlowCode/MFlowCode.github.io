Detailed Description

Per-block fine-grid immersed-boundary state for AMR. A separate module from m_ibm: declaring these derived-type allocatables inside m_ibm corrupts CCE's OpenMP declare-target descriptor table for m_ibm's ghost_points (lib-4425 on its @:ALLOCATE, even for non-AMR runs). Do not fold them back into m_ibm.
