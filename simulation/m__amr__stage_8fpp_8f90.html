module  m_amr_stage
 The AMR hooks of the SSP-RK stage loop (m_time_steppers): the stage-top coarse halo, the fine-level stage, and the end-of-step fold of the fine solution into the level-0 covered cells.

Functions/Subroutines

impure subroutine, public m_amr_stage::s_amr_stage_begin (q_cons)
 Stage top: hoist the cons halo (the AMR fill needs it) so the RHS converts over the buffered domain instead of exchanging a prim halo on the same faces (pointwise, so byte-identical). Only where the cons halo carries everything the prim halo did (no q_T_sf, no igr).
impure subroutine, public m_amr_stage::s_amr_stage_fine (s, t_step, coefs, q_cons, q_t_sf, bc_type, rhs_vf, pb_in, rhs_pb, mv_in, rhs_mv)
 The fine-level stage, with q_cons still the coarse stage-entry state: (1) fill every block's ghosts top-down as per-level waves after one coarse cons halo, (2) overwrite fine-fine seam ghosts with neighbours' stage-entry interiors (posted before the fills, drained after), (3) advance all owned blocks in batches, (4) reflux level-1 blocks into the coarse rhs (one face wave plus one batched apply, order-free). A level>=2 block's coarse side is its parent, refluxed in s_amr_step_fold.
impure subroutine, public m_amr_stage::s_amr_subcycle_fine (q_new, q_old, coefs, q_t_sf, bc_type, pb_in, rhs_pb, mv_in, rhs_mv, t_step)
 Subcycled fine advance (amr_subcycle): after level 0's step (q_old = step-start state, q_new = end state, coarse registers holding its dt-weighted flux integral) level 1 takes two substeps of dt/2. The level-1 patch rings of q_old and q_new are gathered once (s_amr_pc_gather); each stage blends them in time, fills the fine ghosts and advances level 1 with dt/2, accumulating fine registers over both substeps; level 2 (if any) then takes its two substeps (s_amr_subcycle_l2). The registers then correct the coarse state (Berger-Colella) and the caller's fold restricts.
impure subroutine m_amr_stage::s_amr_subcycle_l2 (q_new, coefs, q_t_sf, bc_type, pb_in, rhs_pb, mv_in, rhs_mv, t_step, th_end, dt0)
 Level 2's two substeps of dt0/4 after a level-1 substep ending at coarse time fraction th_end: refill level-1 ghosts at th_end (level-2 parent patches can reach them), fill each level-2 stage from parents blended between their substep-start backup and end state (amr_par_theta), then sync level 2 into level 1.
impure subroutine m_amr_stage::s_amr_fill_levels (q_cons, lev_lo, lev_hi, theta)
 Ghost fill of levels lev_lo..lev_hi: the level-1 wave from the coarse q_cons (or, with theta, the patch cache at that coarse time fraction), then the parent waves. The fine-fine seam reads stage-entry interiors only, so it is posted before the fills and drained after.
impure subroutine m_amr_stage::s_amr_pc_gather (q, t)
 Gather every owned level-1 block's coarse patch ring from q into the pooled amr_cg and keep it as time level t (0: step start, 1: step end), once per coarse step; stages then blend in time with no wave or halo.
impure subroutine m_amr_stage::s_amr_pc_fill (theta)
 Level-1 ghost fill at coarse time fraction theta: blend the cached time levels into the pool, then the batched fill.
impure subroutine m_amr_stage::s_amr_pc_copy (t, theta)
 The used pool (amr_cg_nuse patches) against the cache amr_pc: t >= 0 stores it as time level t (t = 0 sizes the cache); t < 0 writes the pool = (1 - theta) * level 0 + theta * level 1.
impure subroutine, public m_amr_stage::s_amr_step_fold (q_cons)
 End of step: fold the fine solution into the level-0 covered cells (the only deliberate level-0 write) and Berger-Colella state-reflux each level>=2 block into its parent. Bottom-up (per-level waves), so covered cells hold the finest data.
