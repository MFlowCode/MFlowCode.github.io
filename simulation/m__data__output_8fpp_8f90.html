impure subroutine m_data_output::s_write_ib_record (unit, row, gid, fields)
 Write body gid's record in the given row. The format is fixed width by construction: every ES descriptor right-justifies in its field, including for negatives, three-digit exponents, NaN and Inf, so a record is always 10 + 18*size(fields) characters plus its newline. That is what lets a rank write at a computed byte offset, with no gather and no per-rank shards.
