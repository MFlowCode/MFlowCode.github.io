subroutine, private s_compute_ghost_point_pressure (gp, gp_patch_id, radial_vector, vel_ip, rho, pres_ip, pres_gp)
 Ghost pressure from the wall-normal momentum balance, dp/dn = -rho (a_w . n) + rho sum_i kappa_i u_i^2 with n out of the body. a_w is the acceleration of the wall point: the body's translation (force/mass for a two-way coupled body, else the stage-differenced one), alpha x r_w from its angular acceleration, and the centripetal acceleration omega x (omega x r_w) of its rotation about its centroid. A fluid held at an accelerating wall's velocity needs this pressure gradient to stay with it; mirroring the image-point pressure instead leaves the wall layer unbalanced. The second term is the centripetal acceleration of fluid sliding along a curved slip wall, from the image point's velocity relative to the wall; a no-slip wall holds its fluid at the wall velocity, so it has none. Clamped both ways: the linearization holds only while the correction is order one, and an unbounded one drives the ghost state to vacuum.

impure subroutine s_wall_column_setup ()
 Wall columns (patch_ibwall_column): give each first-layer ghost of such a body its gas faces' weights, area times (l(s_f) - l(s_g))/h with l the steady radial conduction coordinate (f_wc_ell) and s the signed wall distance of the face's gas cell and of the ghost. impure subroutine, public s_ibm_advance_wall_columns (q_cons_vf, q_prim_vf, q_t_sf, dt_step)
 Advance every wall column over dt from the state of the step's first RHS, and set the columns' guard targets. impure subroutine, public s_wall_column_tcond (q_cons_vf, q_prim_vf, q_t_sf)
 Conduction temperature of each active column ghost that makes its gas faces carry the column's wall flux, less a one-sided guard: sum_f G_f (T_f - T_cond) = sum_f (q w_f - cut_f), G_f = lambda_f area/h with the face conductivity the diffusion flux forms (each side at its own state, the ghost at T_w), and cut_f = g lambda (T_ext - T_f)^+ area/h. subroutine s_record_gp_surface (i, gp, t_s, mdot_s, reacting)
 Store ghost point i's surface record in gp_surf. The weight is the surface area the point stands for. Ghost points within two cell sizes h = dV^(1/d) of the surface fill a band of volume ~2hA, so dV/(2h) each sums to the area A (a length in 2D) whatever the surface's orientation to the grid. The band lies inside the solid, where a layer at depth s has area A (1 - s/R)^(d-1) on a curved surface; for circles, spheres and cylinders the weight is scaled back by (R/(R - s))^(d-1), which removes that bias (sampled on random lattice offsets: < 0.1% mean, 0.3% spread for a sphere at R = 12h). impure subroutine, public s_update_ib_temperatures (dt_step, q_cons_vf)
 Advance the temperature of each thermal_bc = 3 IB over one step, treating it as one lumped body (small Biot number). With E_solid = m c_s (T - T_ref), the step's face energy in from s_accumulate_ib_face_fluxes and the gasification rate from the surface solve, m c_s dT/dt = Q_in + c_s (T - T_ref) Mdot_out + heat_power - emissivity sigma A (T^4 - T_rad^4). Mdot_out is the solid's mass loss: the face mass sum also carries the ghost cells' non-conservation (a net inflow on a reacting cylinder). T_ref = 298.15 K is the reference of the formation enthalpies, so the gas energy the solid's lost carbon carries is consistent. The step is explicit: the body's thermal time m c_s/(h A) is many orders of magnitude longer than a flow step. For D/ib_heat.dat every body also keeps its gasification rate, face mass out and fluid heat in, Q_in (+ the c_s term for thermal_bc = 3), in patch_ibmdot, mdot_face and qdot.

impure subroutine, public s_conserve_ib_mass (q_cons_vf)

The ghost-cell treatment does not conserve mass at a moving or gasifying wall (face fluxes into overwritten ghost cells against cells changing side); this returns the gas to exactly the gasified mass, lagged by about ib_mass_relax steps.
impure subroutine, public s_add_swept_cells (q_cons_vf, q_start_vf, a_w, dt_step)
 Moving bodies: book the mass and the body-frame energy E' = E - rho u.V_b + rho |V_b|^2/2 of each cell a body covered (+) or uncovered (-) at this stage's placement, into ib_heat(3) (mass out of the body, as a rate) and ib_heat(2) (energy in). The grid faces do not move with the wall, so the face fluxes alone miss the gas swept between face and wall. Called at every placement, after the stage update and before the ghost correction overwrites the covered cells: a cell's content enters the step's result with weight a_w (the product of the later stages' rk_coef(:,1)/rk_coef(:,4)) and the step-start state with 1 - a_w, so it is booked as a_w q + (1 - a_w) q_start. V_b = V + omega x r at the cell, r the minimum-image lever arm to the centroid. A cell passing directly from one body to another is not counted.

impure subroutine, public s_fill_uncovered_cells (q_cons_vf, q_start_vf, q_prim_vf)
 ib_uncover_fill: a cell the body left at this placement (solid at the last one, fluid now) holds the cold ghost state it last had, which the stage update then carries as gas. Give it the mean density, species and pressure of the neighbours in its 3x3(x3) that were fluid at both placements, the state of the flow it moved into; with chemistry the energy is rebuilt from the mean state's temperature. The velocity stays the ghost's: the mean of the neighbours' makes the drag of a moving body disagree more with its Galilean twin. A cell with no such neighbour is left as it is. The step-start state q_start, which the later stages blend in, gets the same state; q_prim_vf (the stage-start primitives) is updated for the image-point interpolation. The halo is filled as well (see the loop bounds). Call before s_add_swept_cells so the booking sees the filled state.

subroutine s_tsc_weights (markers, loc, stencil_center, axis_weights, fluid_frac)
 Triangular-shaped-cloud (quadratic B-spline) weights of a point on the 3x3(x3) stencil centred on the cell nearest to it (Ezra, Peles & Kozak 2025, Int. J. Heat Mass Transf. 252:127314), and the fraction of that weight carried by fluid cells. The kernel is a partition of unity and smooth across cell boundaries, so an interpolated wall quantity converges one order faster than from inverse-distance or bilinear weights, which are only continuous. The weights use the fractional position in the bracketing cell at integer offsets, which keeps the partition of unity exactly on a stretched grid. The stencil is kept inside the halo. Solid cells in it carry the ghost values of the last correction (see ib_keep_ghost); a point buried in a body has no fluid weight.

impure subroutine s_ib_exchange_heat (dt_step)
 Adds ib_heat(1:5) (the host copy: this rank's partial sums per slot) over the ranks that hold each body, through its owner (patch_ibowner_rank), and, with ib_mass_relax, steps the owners' banks. Phase 1: each rank sends its nonzero partials of bodies it does not own to the owner, which adds them in ascending source-rank order.  Largest temperature mirror weight, at most 1, that keeps the ghost consistent with every fluid cell it shares a face with. Conduction across that face is the two-point difference (T_n - T_g)/h, so the line through the ghost (a behind the wall) and the cell (b in front) must not reach the wall beyond T_s: T_g - T_s <= (a/b)(T_s - T_n) on the mirror's side, and T_g = T_s once the cell is past T_s. Ghost state for an immersed surface, from the image-point state and the surface state.

The natural closure is the linear mirror phi_g = 2*phi_s - phi_IP, which is what makes the midpoint of the ghost/image pair reproduce the surface value. Its -1 coefficient on phi_IP is unconditional, though, so it drives strictly positive quantities negative whenever the surface value sits far below the image-point value: a cold wall in hot gas gives a negative ghost temperature and hence a negative ghost density, and a species the surface consumes faster than half the free-stream value gives a negative mass fraction. Rather than clamp the result – which would break sum(Y) = 1 and hide the excursion – the ghost state is the largest convex blend of the mirror back toward the surface value that stays physical: 

phi_g(theta) = phi_s + theta*(phi_s - phi_IP),   theta in [0, 1]

theta = 1 is the full second-order mirror; theta = 0 is the first-order Dirichlet ghost phi_g = phi_s, which is Gibou et al. Each species and the temperature get their own theta (s_blend_ghost_species keeps sum(Y) = 1 through the bath species). Probes and Lagrange weights of the quadratic wall-normal fill of a ghost point. The wall is at s = 0, the probes at s1 and s2 = 2 s1 along the surface normal from the wall foot, the ghost at s = -d (d = |levelset|): 

phi_g = L0 phi_w + L1 phi_1 + L2 phi_2
L0 = (d + s1)(d + s2)/(s1 s2),  L1 = -d (d + s2)/(s1 (s2 - s1)),  L2 = d (d + s1)/(s2 (s2 - s1))
