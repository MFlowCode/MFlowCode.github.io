module  m_species_bounds
 Keeps reconstructed species mass fractions on the simplex (Y >= 0, sum Y = 1). Each cell's face values are first shifted to sum to 1, then scaled toward the cell average by one theta shared by all species and both faces (Zhang & Shu, JCP 2010). With face Y on the simplex the HLLC species fluxes are Y_upwind times the mass flux (Larrouturou, JCP 1991), so they sum to it and keep cell averages non-negative under the Zhang-Shu CFL.

subroutine, public m_species_bounds::s_clean_species (q_cons_vf, tally)
 Fallback (as PeleC's clean_massfrac): clip each rho*Y_k to [0, rho], set rho = sum rho*Y_k, and scale momentum and energy by rho_new/rho_old so velocity and specific energy are kept. A cell whose clipped sum is not positive is left untouched. Every cell is cleaned, but only fluid cells (not IB cells) off by > clean_report_tol*rho, or left untouched, are counted as cell cleanings (per cell per RK stage) for s_report_species_cleanup. tally = F (an AMR fine block, whose markers ib_markers does not hold) cleans without counting.
