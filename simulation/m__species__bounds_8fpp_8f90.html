module  m_species_bounds
 Keeps reconstructed species mass fractions on the simplex (Y >= 0, sum Y = 1). Each cell's face values are first shifted to sum to 1, then scaled toward the cell average by one theta shared by all species and both faces (Zhang & Shu, JCP 2010). With face Y on the simplex the HLLC species fluxes are Y_upwind times the mass flux (Larrouturou, JCP 1991), so they sum to it and keep cell averages non-negative under the Zhang-Shu CFL.

subroutine, public m_species_bounds::s_bound_face_states (q_prim_vf, vl, vr, id)
 Scales each cell's primitive face states vL/vR of direction id toward its cell state by one theta (Zhang & Shu, JCP 2010) so that face rho and p + p_inf stay positive and, with chemistry, the face gas constant stays positive and the face T <= T_surface_max (past which cp can go negative and the sound speed NaN). Inactive where no face state leaves those bounds.
subroutine, public m_species_bounds::s_bound_face_states_zs (q_prim_vf, vl, vr, id)
 face_bound = 2, for stiffened-gas 5- and 6-equation states with one or more fluids (Zhang & Shu, JCP 2010). Each cell's primitive face states vL/vR of direction id are scaled toward its cell state by the largest theta that keeps, on both faces, rho and p + p_inf positive and (p + p_inf)/rho at most face_T_cap times its largest value over the cell and its two neighbours along id. The last bound limits the face sound speed: without it, a face scaled down to a density floor at finite pressure sets an unbounded wave speed. theta is then halved until the conservative interior point (U_bar - w(U_L + U_R))/(1 - 2w), with w the Gauss-Lobatto end weight, has positive density and pressure. Volume fractions get their own theta into [0, 1]. The limiter is inactive wherever every bound already holds. Cell averages then stay positive for dt sum_d max(|u_d| + c)/dx_d <= w.

subroutine, public m_species_bounds::s_clean_species (q_cons_vf, lvl)
 Fallback (as PeleC's clean_massfrac): clip each rho*Y_k to [0, rho], set rho = sum rho*Y_k, and scale momentum and energy by rho_new/rho_old so velocity and specific energy are kept. A cell whose clipped sum is not positive is left untouched. Every cell is cleaned, but only fluid cells (not IB cells) off by > clean_report_tol*rho, or left untouched, are counted as cell cleanings (per cell per RK stage) for s_report_species_cleanup, under AMR level lvl (0 = the coarse grid). A fine block's markers are in ib_markers_fine.
