Modules

module  m_amr_registers
 AMR flux registers: per-RK-stage refluxing at the coarse/fine block boundary. Depends only on m_derived_types and m_global_parameters so m_rhs (capture) and m_time_steppers (apply) can use it without cycles. creg is indexed 0-based relative to the rank's block intersection; freg 0-based local fine (children of coarse cell t are 2*t, 2*t+1). The slot dimension grows on demand (s_amr_reg_reserve). The captured flux is the 1:sys_size advective flux of flux_rsx_vf plus the viscous, species-diffusion and conduction fluxes of flux_src_rsx_vf; the non-conservative alpha*div(u) remainder is not captured (held by mpp_lim).

impure subroutine, public m_amr_registers::s_amr_reg_prepare ()
 Build the dense participation-local register index (amr_reg_of/amr_reg_n) and size and zero the registers. Keyed on the mesh epoch (block-count change is the tripwire). Block g maps iff this rank owns g or g's parent, or reflux-face-participates in g per s_amr_reflux_faces, so the map covers every capture, receive and apply that indexes g's slot. Dense slots alias across rebuilds, hence the zeroing; contents are dead at a rebuild because stage 1 overwrites them.

impure subroutine, public m_amr_registers::s_amr_apply_reflux (rhs_vf)
 Correct the coarse rhs in the first cell outside each block face so the update sees the child-averaged fine flux: the low face is the outside cell's right face (rhs += (F_coarse - Fbar_fine)/dx), the high face its left face (rhs += (Fbar_fine - F_coarse)/dx). c1/c2 are relative 0-based coarse transverse indices. CCE 19.0.0: two or more of the per-direction loop nests in one procedure fail (ftn-7991 on offload, bad code on CPU), so each lives in its own procedure (s_amr_apply_reflux_d1/d2/d3); keep them separate.

impure subroutine, public m_amr_registers::s_amr_reflux_apply_faces (q, islot, rr, dtl, olo, ohi, glo, ghi, woff, w_lo, w_hi, mlo, mhi)
 Berger-Colella state reflux: q(outside) += w*dtl*(F_coarse - Fbar_fine)/m on the low face and the negative on the high face, per active dim, with F_coarse = creg and Fbar_fine = freg averaged over the rr**(ndim-1) covering fine faces. Framing is caller-passed: islot dense register slot; olo/ohi outside coarse-cell index below/above the face; glo/ghi creg-local loop range; woff transverse write origin; w_lo/w_hi per-face weight; mlo/mhi outside-cell width. A zero weight skips the write because the outside index may be out of bounds on an unowned face.
