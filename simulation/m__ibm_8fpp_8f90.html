subroutine, private m_ibm::s_compute_ghost_point_pressure (gp, gp_patch_id, radial_vector, vel_ip, rho, pres_ip, pres_gp)
 Ghost pressure from the wall-normal momentum balance, dp/dn = -rho (a_w . n) + rho sum_i kappa_i u_i^2 with n out of the body. a_w is the acceleration of the wall point: the body's translation (force/mass for a two-way coupled body, else the stage-differenced one), alpha x r_w from its angular acceleration, and the centripetal acceleration omega x (omega x r_w) of its rotation about its centroid. A fluid held at an accelerating wall's velocity needs this pressure gradient to stay with it; mirroring the image-point pressure instead leaves the wall layer unbalanced. The second term is the centripetal acceleration of fluid sliding along a curved slip wall, from the image point's velocity relative to the wall; a no-slip wall holds its fluid at the wall velocity, so it has none. Clamped both ways: the linearization holds only while the correction is order one, and an unbounded one drives the ghost state to vacuum.

subroutine m_ibm::s_record_gp_surface (i, gp, t_s, mdot_s, reacting)
 Store ghost point i's surface record in gp_surf. The weight is the surface area the point stands for. Ghost points within two cell sizes h = dV^(1/d) of the surface fill a band of volume ~2hA, so dV/(2h) each sums to the area A (a length in 2D) whatever the surface's orientation to the grid. The band lies inside the solid, where a layer at depth s has area A (1 - s/R)^(d-1) on a curved surface; for circles, spheres and cylinders the weight is scaled back by (R/(R - s))^(d-1), which removes that bias (sampled on random lattice offsets: < 0.1% mean, 0.3% spread for a sphere at R = 12h). impure subroutine, public m_ibm::s_update_ib_temperatures (dt_step, q_cons_vf)
 Advance the temperature of each thermal_bc = 3 IB over one step, treating it as one lumped body (small Biot number). With E_solid = m c_s (T - T_ref), the step's face energy in from s_accumulate_ib_face_fluxes and the gasification rate from the surface solve, m c_s dT/dt = Q_in + c_s (T - T_ref) Mdot_out + heat_power - emissivity sigma A (T^4 - T_rad^4). Mdot_out is the solid's mass loss: the face mass sum also carries the ghost cells' non-conservation (a net inflow on a reacting cylinder). T_ref = 298.15 K is the reference of the formation enthalpies, so the gas energy the solid's lost carbon carries is consistent. The step is explicit: the body's thermal time m c_s/(h A) is many orders of magnitude longer than a flow step. For D/ib_heat.dat every body also keeps its gasification rate, face mass out and fluid heat in, Q_in (+ the c_s term for thermal_bc = 3), in patch_ibmdot, mdot_face and qdot.

impure subroutine, public m_ibm::s_add_swept_cells (q_cons_vf, q_start_vf, a_w, dt_step)
 Moving bodies: book the mass and the body-frame energy E' = E - rho u.V_b + rho |V_b|^2/2 of each cell a body covered (+) or uncovered (-) at this stage's placement, into ib_heat(3) (mass out of the body, as a rate) and ib_heat(2) (energy in). The grid faces do not move with the wall, so the face fluxes alone miss the gas swept between face and wall. Called at every placement, after the stage update and before the ghost correction overwrites the covered cells: a cell's content enters the step's result with weight a_w (the product of the later stages' rk_coef(:,1)/rk_coef(:,4)) and the step-start state with 1 - a_w, so it is booked as a_w q + (1 - a_w) q_start. V_b = V + omega x r at the cell, r the minimum-image lever arm to the centroid. A cell passing directly from one body to another is not counted.

impure subroutine, public m_ibm::s_fill_uncovered_cells (q_cons_vf, q_start_vf, q_prim_vf)
 ib_uncover_fill: a cell the body left at this placement (solid at the last one, fluid now) holds the cold ghost state it last had, which the stage update then carries as gas. Give it the mean density, species and pressure of the neighbours in its 3x3(x3) that were fluid at both placements, the state of the flow it moved into; with chemistry the energy is rebuilt from the mean state's temperature. The velocity stays the ghost's: the mean of the neighbours' makes the drag of a moving body disagree more with its Galilean twin. A cell with no such neighbour is left as it is. subroutine m_ibm::s_tsc_weights (markers, loc, stencil_center, axis_weights, fluid_frac)
 Triangular-shaped-cloud (quadratic B-spline) weights of a point on the 3x3(x3) stencil centred on the cell nearest to it (Ezra, Peles & Kozak 2025, Int. J. Heat Mass Transf. 252:127314), and the fraction of that weight carried by fluid cells. The kernel is a partition of unity and smooth across cell boundaries, so an interpolated wall quantity converges one order faster than from inverse-distance or bilinear weights, which are only continuous. The weights use the fractional position in the bracketing cell at integer offsets, which keeps the partition of unity exactly on a stretched grid. The stencil is kept inside the halo. Solid cells in it carry the ghost values of the last correction (see ib_keep_ghost); a point buried in a body has no fluid weight.

subroutine m_ibm::s_accumulate_ib_face_fluxes (idir, stage, q_cons_vf, q_prim_vf, markers)
 Add direction idir's fluid-body face fluxes to each IB. These are the fluxes the RHS differences, Riemann (flux_rsx_vf) plus viscous, capillary, conductive and diffusive (flux_src_rsx_vf), so each body receives exactly what the fluid loses. A face counts on the rank owning its fluid cell. With IB forces: force (1:3) and torque about the centroid (4:6) of each local IB into ib_face_ft, from the last RHS evaluation (idir = 1 starts afresh); a moving body's momentum flux is taken through a face moving with its wall (face momentum times the wall's normal velocity is removed). With ib_exchange: energy in and mass out of each lumped body (every body with ib_heat_wrt) into ib_heat(2:3), weighted so a step's stages sum to its exchange (SSP-RK: 1; 1/2, 1/2; 1/6, 1/6, 2/3).

impure subroutine m_ibm::s_ib_exchange_heat (dt_step)
 Adds ib_heat(1:5) (the host copy: this rank's partial sums per slot) over the ranks that hold each body, through its owner (patch_ibowner_rank), and, with ib_mass_relax, steps the owners' banks. Phase 1: each rank sends its nonzero partials of bodies it does not own to the owner, which adds them in ascending source-rank order. Phase 2: each owner returns the totals and the bank's release (row 7) to the ranks that sent, the only ranks with cells of the body. Both are point-to-point with neighborhood ranks and carry only bodies a rank shares, so the volume follows the bodies on rank boundaries, not the body count.

real(wp) function m_ibm::f_face_theta (q_prim_vf, markers, gp, t_ip, t_s)
 Largest temperature mirror weight, at most 1, that keeps the ghost consistent with every fluid cell it shares a face with. Conduction across that face is the two-point difference (T_n - T_g)/h, so the line through the ghost (a behind the wall) and the cell (b in front) must not reach the wall beyond T_s: T_g - T_s <= (a/b)(T_s - T_n) on the mirror's side, and T_g = T_s once the cell is past T_s. The image point sits about a cell out, so without this an under-resolved layer leaves the mirror set by the second gas cell and drives the first past the wall temperature. A resolved, convex profile puts the mirror inside the bound, so it leaves the mirror untouched there. A cell closer than a quarter of its spacing to the wall plane is left out: its temperature is within noise of T_s, and the bound, whose slope there is a/b, then switched theta between 0 and 1 from step to step and kept a steady conduction annulus from converging.
