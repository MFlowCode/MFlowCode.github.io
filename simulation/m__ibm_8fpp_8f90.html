subroutine m_ibm::s_record_gp_surface (i, gp, t_s, mdot_s, reacting)
 Store ghost point i's surface record in gp_surf. The weight is the surface area the point stands for. Ghost points within two cell sizes h = dV^(1/d) of the surface fill a band of volume ~2hA, so dV/(2h) each sums to the area A (a length in 2D) whatever the surface's orientation to the grid. The band lies inside the solid, where a layer at depth s has area A (1 - s/R)^(d-1) on a curved surface; for circles, spheres and cylinders the weight is scaled back by (R/(R - s))^(d-1), which removes that bias (sampled on random lattice offsets: < 0.1% mean, 0.3% spread for a sphere at R = 12h). The factor is limited to R/(R - s) <= 2, so a body of only a few cells cannot give an infinite or negative weight.

subroutine m_ibm::s_tsc_weights (markers, loc, ic, wd, fluid_frac)
 Triangular-shaped-cloud (quadratic B-spline) weights of a point on the 3x3(x3) stencil centred on the cell nearest to it (Ezra, Peles & Kozak 2025, Int. J. Heat Mass Transf. 252:127314), and the fraction of that weight carried by fluid cells. The kernel is a partition of unity and smooth across cell boundaries, so an interpolated wall quantity converges one order faster than from inverse-distance or bilinear weights, which are only continuous. The weights use the fractional position in the bracketing cell at integer offsets, which keeps the partition of unity exactly on a stretched grid. The stencil is kept inside the halo. Solid cells in it carry the ghost values of the last correction (see ib_keep_ghost); a point buried in a body has no fluid weight.

 Ghost value from the quadratic wall-normal profile, bounded by the linear mirror (minmod). subroutine m_ibm::s_prescribed_kinematics (i, t)
 Prescribed kinematics for patch i at time t: hinged flapping (kin_model = 1) or the Eldredge pitch ramp (kin_model = 2). Flapping rolls phi about the lab x axis through the hinge and pitches theta about the body spanwise (y) axis through it; the ramp holds phi at zero and drives theta alone. Both compose as R = Rx(phi) Ry(theta) (MFC's angle convention) and set the angles, centroid, velocity and lab-frame angular velocity; nothing is integrated, so restarts are exact.
subroutine m_ibm::s_accumulate_ib_face_fluxes (idir)
 Add direction idir's fluid-body face fluxes to ib_face_ft: force (1:3) and torque about the centroid (4:6) on each local IB. These are the momentum fluxes the RHS differences, Riemann (flux_rsx_vf) plus viscous and capillary (flux_src_rsx_vf), so the body receives exactly the momentum the fluid loses. A face counts on the rank owning its fluid cell; idir = 1 starts afresh.
