m_amr_registers Module Reference

AMR flux registers: per-RK-stage refluxing at the coarse/fine block boundary. Depends only on m_derived_types and m_global_parameters so m_rhs (capture) and m_time_steppers (apply) can use it without cycles. creg is indexed 0-based relative to the rank's block intersection; freg 0-based local fine (children of coarse cell t are 2*t, 2*t+1). The slot dimension grows on demand (s_amr_reg_reserve). The captured flux is the 1:sys_size advective flux of flux_rsx_vf plus the viscous, species-diffusion and conduction fluxes of flux_src_rsx_vf; the non-conservative alpha*div(u) remainder is not captured (held by mpp_lim). Detailed Description

AMR flux registers: per-RK-stage refluxing at the coarse/fine block boundary. Depends only on m_derived_types and m_global_parameters so m_rhs (capture) and m_time_steppers (apply) can use it without cycles. creg is indexed 0-based relative to the rank's block intersection; freg 0-based local fine (children of coarse cell t are 2*t, 2*t+1). The slot dimension grows on demand (s_amr_reg_reserve). The captured flux is the 1:sys_size advective flux of flux_rsx_vf plus the viscous, species-diffusion and conduction fluxes of flux_src_rsx_vf; the non-conservative alpha*div(u) remainder is not captured (held by mpp_lim).
